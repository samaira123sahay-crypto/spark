What SparkTG is, in three lines

SparkTG is a cloud communication platform for growing businesses. In plain words, it covers three things.

Calls

A system, so your team takes calls without phone hardware.

WhatsApp

Messages sent and received from your business through the , such as order updates.

Assistants

A that answers routine questions by voice or text, at any hour.

Who it's for: businesses that talk to many customers every day. What they buy: a platform they use daily, not a one-off service.

What you get

The products SparkTG lists on its site, each described in plain words.

Cloud contact centre

SparkTG VCC, a . Your agents take and make calls from anywhere.

Call menu

The IVR solution: the menu callers hear first ("press 1 for…"), which sends each one to the right place.

WhatsApp for business

Automated and two-way WhatsApp messages, including media and order updates, through the Business API.

Voice and chat assistants

Voice bots and chat bots that answer routine questions so your team doesn't have to.

Call masking

Hides real phone numbers between customer and agent.

Click to call
